markets

A small Jupyter notebook I keep for pulling market and macro data with yfinance, pandas and plotly, exported here as two interactive charts.

The first is an animated bar chart of the largest economies ranked by GDP, stepping through one year at a time. Below it, a longer export collects a few exploratory figures: Apple’s share price and market capitalisation over recent years, and the same GDP data shown as a race chart and as a table.
